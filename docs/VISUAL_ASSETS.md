# Visual asset replacements

The catalogue category illustrations in `public/assets/categories/` are original, project-owned SVG artwork created for The Barrel House. They provide stable local visuals and do not depict or imply photography of a real brand or product.

The current product records may still contain externally hosted reference images. Before production, the owner should replace them with approved, licensed product photography or original product packaging images. Keep approved files under `public/assets/products/`, update each product's `image` path, and retain descriptive alt text. Generic category artwork is the approved visual fallback until those files are supplied.
